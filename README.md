# Vectra App

Track your expenses, income, and budget on the go with Vectra App

An app that lets you track money in and money out, and helps you manage your budget. It provides you with insights and analytics to help you make informed decisions. Also, assist in planning your budget and saving money.

Has Categories (Kategoria) and Subcategories (Semikategoria) to help you organize your expenses and income.

Under these categories, you can add your expenses and income.

A category can be: Family, Personal, Business, etc.

A subcategory can be: Food, Transport, etc.
